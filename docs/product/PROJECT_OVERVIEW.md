# SARA — Smart Accountability & Resolution Assistant

## Project Overview

### What SARA Is

SARA is an **AI-powered accountability and escalation layer** designed to operate alongside existing public grievance redressal systems. It does not replace systems like CPGRAMS — it enhances them by adding proactive monitoring, predictive risk intelligence, explainable escalation, evidence-aware resolution verification, and a complete audit trail for every grievance lifecycle event.

### What SARA Is NOT

| SARA is NOT | SARA IS |
|---|---|
| A CPGRAMS replacement | An interoperable accountability layer that augments existing systems |
| A generic complaint portal | A post-submission intelligence engine |
| A chatbot | A governance engine with deterministic policy enforcement |
| An AI judge | A recommendation and risk-signaling system |
| An autonomous disciplinary system | A human-in-the-loop accountability framework |
| A simple CRUD ticketing system | An event-driven, state-machine-governed workflow platform |

### The Central Question

SARA continuously answers:

> **After a grievance enters the system, is the machinery of government actually working to resolve it — and can we prove it?**

Specifically:

- Is the grievance correctly classified?
- Is it routed to the correct authority?
- Who is responsible?
- Has the responsible officer acted?
- Is the complaint approaching an SLA breach?
- Is the workflow showing signs of delay?
- Does the resolution claim have sufficient evidence?
- Did the citizen actually confirm the resolution?
- Should the grievance be reopened or escalated?
- Can a supervisor understand exactly why a case was escalated?

### Core Intelligence Loop

```
DETECT → EXPLAIN → ESCALATE → VERIFY
```

1. **DETECT**: AI continuously monitors grievance workflows for risk signals — inactivity, SLA proximity, pattern anomalies, missing evidence
2. **EXPLAIN**: Every risk detection comes with a human-readable explanation grounded in specific data points
3. **ESCALATE**: Deterministic governance policies (not AI) trigger escalation based on configurable rules
4. **VERIFY**: Resolution claims are verified through evidence analysis and citizen confirmation before closure

### Product Vision

> "Existing grievance systems manage complaints. SARA adds an intelligent accountability layer that continuously monitors the grievance lifecycle, predicts failures, explains risk, triggers policy-driven escalation, verifies resolution, and maintains an auditable chain of events across interoperable systems."

### Strongest Product Loop

```
COMPLAINT → UNDERSTAND → ROUTE → MONITOR → PREDICT → WARN → ESCALATE → VERIFY → REOPEN/CLOSE → AUDIT
```

### Long-Term Positioning

SARA is positioned as **B2G/GovTech accountability infrastructure** that can serve:

- Central government organizations
- State governments
- Departments and ministries
- Municipal bodies
- Public-sector organizations

The architecture is designed to be tenant-aware and policy-configurable from inception, even though multi-tenancy is not an MVP deliverable.
